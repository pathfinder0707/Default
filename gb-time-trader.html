Framework reference
Reads — for Zurich HH:MM: MM (primary, wins the label), HH+MM, |HH−MM|. All three checked independently, ±tolerance.
GB primes — 3 11 17 29 41 47 53 59 71 83 89 97
CE alternates — 7 14 23 35 44 50 56 65 77 · 00 is a valid node.
Algo 1 forward — 00 → 11/14 → 41/44 → 03/7 → 17/23 → 71/77 or 29/35 ⊣
Algo 2 reverse — 29/35 → 47/53 → 11/14 → 17/23 → 59/65 → 03/7 ⊣ (index decreases)
Minimum travel — the nearest upcoming node in clock time across live candidates.
Nodes are decision levels — price holds them as support, holds them as resistance, or breaks through. Neither algo is inherently bullish or bearish.

Time Dilation Theory

Can the TDT candle count be traded? A full evaluation of the method's mechanics, an exhaustive parameter search, and an assessment of the one effect that survived — including whether it survives having been searched for.

02Mandate and hypotheses

TDT counts candles forward from a swing pivot and treats the numbers 7, 13 and 21 as decision points. Two trade models are built on it: Model #2 reads a completed leg by the count it terminated on ([1-7] continuation, [1-13] reversal inside), and Model #3 grades three nested counts, refusing any signature containing a 21.

That is falsifiable, and this review tests it as four separate claims, each of which could hold while the others fail:
